Indian Railways & IRCTC Ticketing Guide

Travel Classes Explained:

  • 1A (AC First Class): Luxury 2-berth coupes and 4-berth cabins with lockable doors, plush bedding, and tailored meals.
  • 2A (AC 2-Tier): Air-conditioned coach with 2-level berths (Upper & Lower) and curtain privacy.
  • 3A (AC 3-Tier): Most popular AC travel tier with 3-level berths (Lower, Middle, Upper) and side berths.
  • SL (Sleeper Class): Classic non-AC open-window coach with 3-tier berths, economical for long-distance journeys.
  • CC (AC Chair Car): Comfortable 3x2 cushioned seating for daytime intercity trains (Shatabdi, Vande Bharat, Intercity).
  • EC (Executive Chair Car): Ultra-spacious 2x2 rotating seats with premium onboard catering.
  • 2S (Second Sitting): Budget reserved non-AC seat for short hops.

Quotas Decoded:

  • GN (General Quota): Standard booking open 120 days in advance.
  • TQ (Tatkal Quota): Emergency quota open 1 day in advance at 10:00 AM (AC) and 11:00 AM (Non-AC).
  • PT (Premium Tatkal Quota): Dynamic pricing quota based on instant demand.
  • LD (Ladies Quota): Reserved berths for solo female travelers and mothers traveling with children.
  • SS (Senior Citizen Quota): Lower berth preference reserved for senior citizens.
